## Creative Direction
- Tone preset: polished
- Creative direction: Architectural mission control meets high-stakes financial terminal
- Interpretation: Restrained, authoritative, sharp layout, warm drafting paper aesthetic, crisp animations without silly fluff.
- Angle: Multi-crore construction sites require the precision of an aerospace launchpad.
- Hook: Live site sensors ticking in real-time on a blueprint drafting canvas.
- Outro / punchline: "Civil engineering moves fast. Now your telemetry moves faster."
- Avoid:
  - Generic SaaS language
  - Abstract filler visuals
  - Low contrast or unreadable text
